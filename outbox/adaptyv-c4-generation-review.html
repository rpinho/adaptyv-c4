Evidence and limits

REVIEW DRAFT FOR RICARDO — NOT SUBMITTED

Open-weight RFD4-Proteina generated four arms of 200 human TfR1 apical-domain binder poses each, with inline geometric metrics. One unconditioned arm and His-prior 0.10, 0.20 and 0.30 arms used the same target/epitope, seed and generation settings. The per-arm top-20 geometry pool was rigidly restored to the complete observed human receptor dimer using unchanged native-context checks. All 80 passed those geometric checks. The final 20 were selected by explicit His-N/basic-receptor proximity hypotheses, then hotspot/clash/bond geometry, with at most three sequences per declared family.

His contacts are descriptive distances in generated poses against observed native sidechain rotamers; they do not measure protonation, affinity, its direction of change or pH selectivity. The 4.5 A contact convention is not a validated functional threshold. Priors are soft, not achieved sequence frequencies. Seven selected sequences contain at least 30% His; this is a descriptive composition flag, not a validated developability cutoff. Many geometry tuples tie, so lexical source-ID ordering can determine tied ranks. The family rule is sequence-based and does not establish structural independence.

Outstanding: no independent complex refolding, interface ddG/His scan, biochemical pH binding measurement, expression/developability validation or mouse binding validation.
