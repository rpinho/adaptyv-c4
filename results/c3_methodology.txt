We combined three complementary approaches to design de novo single-chain binders against the GLP1R extracellular domain (ECD), targeting the hydrophobic groove that natively accommodates GLP-1's N-terminal helix.

Approach 1 — BindCraft (8 designs): We used BindCraft with forced targeting on the GLP1R ECD from PDB 6X18 (chain R). Hotspot residues were selected to cover the groove: W33, W39, R40, E68, Y69, W91 and surrounding positions (A67-A69, A88-A91, A121, A123, A128, A137-A145). The campaign explored binder lengths 45-100 residues over 3000 trajectories, yielding 10 ranked designs. Two were dropped: one for excessive steric clashes (13 all-atom clashes), one for marginal ESMFold refold confidence (pLDDT 68.4). The remaining 8 designs span iPTM 0.76-0.85, AF2 pLDDT 0.83-0.93, with full contact across all 6 key groove residues.

Approach 2 — Point-mutation variants (7 designs): Structural analysis of the top 3 BindCraft designs identified interface-edge positions where charge optimization could improve binding. We introduced E→Q, Q→K, and F→Y substitutions at positions near the binding interface to move net charge closer to neutral while preserving or improving contacts with groove residues. Each variant differs by 1-2 mutations from its parent. Variant count was reduced from 9 to 7 after review recommended trading correlated variants for diverse backbones.

Approach 3 — RFD4-Proteina (6 designs): We used NVIDIA's RFD4-Proteina diffusion model as an orthogonal generation method, targeting the same GLP1R ECD groove with hotspot conditioning on W33, W39, R40, E68, Y69, W91, Y88, and R121. 200 designs were generated, filtered to exclude transmembrane-like sequences (max 19-residue Kyte-Doolittle window < 1.6, GRAVY < 0.3) and require sufficient charged fraction (>15%) for soluble expression. Six designs were selected across three rounds: 3 by charge balance and hydropathy, 2 specifically selected for high Trp/Tyr content (9-12%) to provide aromatic complementarity for the W33/W39/Y69-lined groove, and 1 replacing a low-WY design (1.5% WY) with a Trp/Tyr-enriched alternative (11.5% WY, 2W+4Y).

All 20 designs were independently validated using ESMFold (facebook/esmfold_v1) single-sequence structure prediction, achieving refold pLDDT 80.3-93.5. Independent biophysical review by dual AI agents screened for extreme charge, tandem repeats, aggregation-prone hydrophobicity, poly-amino-acid runs, and transmembrane-like segments. Design lengths range from 52-100 residues with net charges from -10 to 0. The final portfolio has a mean Trp+Tyr fraction of 6.7%, with 3 designs above 9% WY for aromatic groove complementarity.
