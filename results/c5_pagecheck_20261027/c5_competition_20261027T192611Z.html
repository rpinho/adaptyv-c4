Frequently asked questions

Do I have to use Claude?

You can enter the open track with any design method. Using Claude for competition-related work is a condition of receiving Anthropic support in Tracks 1 and 2.

How do the five challenge windows work?

A new challenge opens every Monday and stays open for two weeks, so consecutive challenges overlap. Each closes at 23:59 Anywhere on Earth (AoE, UTC−12) on its last day. Challenge 1: Sep 28 – Oct 7. Challenge 2: Oct 5 – 18. Challenge 3: Oct 12 – 25. Challenge 4: Oct 19 – Nov 1. Challenge 5: Oct 26 – Nov 8. Submissions for a challenge close when its window ends.

Will every submission be tested?

No. Anthropic and Adaptyv will select designs for wet-lab testing using a Claude-based workflow. The selection prompt is public, with each challenge's parameters, at proteinbase.com/competitions/anthropic-adaptyv-2026/selection. Selection will not rely on a single in silico metric. All designs must be reviewed by the submitting researcher before submission. We encourage participants to submit the designs they think will fare best experimentally and to describe their methods and design choices in detail. Teams selected for Track 1 have a reserved testing allocation of up to 20 designs per challenge. Tracks 2 and 3 have no guaranteed allocation.

    Who owns the designs, and what becomes public?

    Participants retain ownership of their designs. For tested designs, results are published openly on Proteinbase, including sequences, predicted structures, design methods, and experimental measurements. Negative results are published too, so the whole community can learn from the experiments.

    Is there a prize?

    The competition won’t have a cash prize, but there will be special non-cash prizes. We’ll announce the details soon.
