## Verbatim tweet text

> Our last challenge in the Anthropic x Adaptyv Protein Design Competition is about immunotherapy, one of the most important ways of treating cancer.
>
> Immunotherapy helps the immune system attack cancer cells, but distinguishing them from healthy cells isn't always easy. In some cancers, the difference can be as subtle as a single amino acid in a small protein fragment.
>
> That's the case with KRAS, a protein that works like a growth switch in our cells. In many cancers, a mutation leaves the switch stuck on, and the cell keeps multiplying itself and grows into a tumor.
>
> In Challenge 5, you need to design a binder that sticks to the mutated piece of KRAS, but not to the healthy version, so that the immune system can find and attack the cancer cells.
>
> The deadline to submit protein designs for this challenge is November 8. Then, we'll pick the best designs and test them in our lab.
